      How to play

      Answer six football questions. Each question has several valid answers. Every correct answer earns 25–100 km, with more distance for rarer answers.

      Each answer sinks to its score on the 0–100 depth scale: 0 stays at the surface, 100 reaches the ocean floor. Depth is a visual representation of points, not a real-world distance. Add up all six answers to reach the 400 km expedition target. You have 30 seconds per question. An unaccepted name does not submit an answer: keep trying while the same timer runs. When time runs out, the game moves on without awarding points. A new round unlocks at 00:00 UTC while scheduled rounds are available. Rarity tiers are preset by the game, not calculated from live player percentages. The answer key and sources appear after the round ends. Restart is available during or after a round and starts a fresh practice attempt without changing your ranked attempt.
